Editorial Standard

Professionalism, excellence, security, and quality

Every article should help a buyer, integrator, technician, or project owner make a better field decision. Recommendations must be tied to site requirements, approved scope, manufacturer guidance, and applicable codes.

Core Topics

Field knowledge built around commercial deployment

CCTV

Camera placement starts with the security objective

Coverage should be planned around entrances, transaction areas, loading zones, circulation paths, identification needs, lighting, mounting constraints, retention requirements, and privacy considerations. Camera count alone is not a design standard.

Structured Cabling

Good cabling is designed for the handoff

Pathways, bend radius, separation, labeling, patching, rack organization, testing, and closeout records affect maintainability long after installation. A clean handoff reduces uncertainty for the next technician.

Network Security

Segmentation reduces unnecessary exposure

Separating corporate, guest, IoT, voice, management, and surveillance traffic can improve control and troubleshooting. The exact VLAN, firewall, and access policy should match the environment and operational requirements.

Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi upgrades require a controlled cutover

Document the existing SSIDs, authentication, addressing, reservations, VLANs, dependencies, and rollback path before replacing equipment. Redundancy and maintenance windows can reduce disruption, but no public website should promise an interruption-free change without understanding the site.
